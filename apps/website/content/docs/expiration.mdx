title: Expiration and limits

## Planned lifetime options

The settings interface offers **5 minutes**, **10 minutes** (default),
**30 minutes**, **1 hour**, and **Until stopped**. The Rust backend will be
authoritative for expiration; the interface's countdown is informational.

Until stopped means no scheduled deadline. It does not mean a permanent link:
revocation, full application exit, or a restart will invalidate the in-memory share.

## Download limits

An optional positive download limit is intended to make a share unavailable after
its allowed download reservations are used. A count of one is not proof that the
recipient saved the file successfully.

The backend's exact treatment of aborted transfers, retries, HEAD, and range requests
must be finalized and tested in the core-sharing issue. The preview's counters
demonstrate the interface only and cannot establish secure limit enforcement.

## Stop sharing early

Use **Stop sharing** in share details or the share's action menu. The intended
behavior blocks new page/download requests immediately after revocation. In-flight
transfer cancellation policy still requires backend implementation and testing.

Expired and stopped shares may remain in the list for clarity. Sharing again
creates a fresh share and token; it does not reactivate the old URL.

## Start over safely

Settings and profiles are intended to persist; active shares and tokens are not.
After a full restart, recipients need fresh links. The current build does not yet
provide verified sharing persistence, server enforcement, or background lifecycle.
